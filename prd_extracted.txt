1. Overview
Vitality is a mobile wellness companion designed for senior members to manage daily nutrition, hydration, light-impact exercise, medication reminders, and long-term health progress in one place. The product surfaces a small number of daily actions — log a meal, log water, start a suggested exercise, check vitals — inside a calm, high-contrast, low-friction interface suited to older adults, some of whom may be managing chronic conditions or mobility limitations.
This PRD documents the requirements for five core mobile screens currently represented in design mockups: the Daily Dashboard, Daily Meals & Photos, Suggested Exercises, More Suggested Exercises, and the Progress Dashboard.
1.1 Problem Statement
Seniors often struggle to keep a consistent, safe, low-impact fitness routine without personalized guidance.
Nutrition and hydration tracking tools on the market are complex, dense, and not designed for older or vision-impaired users.
Families and caregivers lack a simple, at-a-glance view of a senior's daily health activity and milestones.
Existing fitness apps assume high-intensity goals (step counts, calorie deficits) that are inappropriate or discouraging for the senior demographic.
1.2 Goals & Objectives
Provide a single daily home screen summarizing meals, exercise, hydration, and vitals for the day.
Recommend safe, low-impact exercises tailored to senior mobility and fitness levels.
Make meal and hydration logging effortless (photo-based logging, one-tap water add).
Visualize weekly/monthly progress and celebrate milestones to reinforce adherence.
Provide fast access to safety-critical actions (medications, heart rate, emergency).
1.3 Target Users
Primary: Senior members (65+) managing their own wellness routine independently.
Secondary: Adult children / caregivers who may monitor or assist with the account.
Tertiary: Care coordinators at senior-living or wellness programs (future consideration).

3. Navigation & Information Architecture
All primary screens share a persistent left-hand navigation rail (desktop/tablet) or bottom tab bar (mobile) with the following top-level destinations, consistent with the mockups:
Dashboard — Home / Daily Dashboard
Exercises — Suggested Exercises and extended catalog
Meals — Daily Meals & Photos
Progress — Progress Dashboard
Settings — account and app preferences
The member's name, membership tier ("Senior Member"), and avatar are surfaced consistently in the top-right header across all screens, alongside notification and account icons.

5. Non-Functional Requirements
5.1 Accessibility
Minimum body text size equivalent to 16pt with support for OS-level dynamic type / font scaling.
Color contrast must meet WCAG 2.1 AA at minimum, given the older-adult user base.
All interactive targets (buttons, cards) minimum 44x44pt tap area.
Status/difficulty indicators must not rely on color alone — pair with text or icon.
Full screen-reader (VoiceOver/TalkBack) support for all primary flows.
5.2 Performance
Dashboard and Progress screens should load cached data in under 1 second, with live sync completing within 3 seconds on a standard connection.
Photo-based meal logging should support offline capture with background sync when connectivity resumes.
5.3 Privacy & Security
Health, meal-photo, and biometric data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
Caregiver access to a member's data requires explicit, revocable member consent.
Compliant with applicable health-data privacy regulations for the target market (e.g., HIPAA considerations where applicable).
5.4 Reliability
Emergency quick action must function even in degraded network conditions (e.g., fallback to device-level emergency call).
Logged data (meals, exercise, hydration) must not be lost on app crash or backgrounding; use local persistence with sync-on-reconnect.

7. Assumptions & Constraints
Members have a compatible smartphone (iOS 15+ / Android 10+) and basic camera access permissions.
Exercise content (video/instructions) is either produced in-house or licensed from a certified senior-fitness content provider.
Calorie and nutrition estimation from meal photos may rely on a third-party food-recognition API; accuracy is best-effort, not medical-grade.
Medication reminders integrate with data the member enters manually; the app does not currently verify prescriptions.
7.1 Out of Scope (this release)
Telehealth / live video consultation with clinicians.
Wearable device integration (e.g., smartwatches) — planned for a future phase.
Social / community features (friends, leaderboards).
Caregiver companion app (dedicated caregiver-side experience).
8. Open Questions
What data source powers step counts — phone sensors, manual entry, or future wearable integration?
What is the exact behavior of the Emergency action — direct 911/emergency-services call, caregiver alert, or both?
Who defines the personalized daily exercise/calorie/water goals — a clinician, onboarding questionnaire, or configurable defaults?
Is caregiver account linking in scope for this release or a fast-follow?
